## The Challenge

When working with power tools without a permanent workshop, sawdust generation becomes a constant issue. Portable vacuum cleaners have hoses of 1 1/4″ (32 mm), whereas the tool inlets are designed for 1 7/8″ (48 mm) hoses. No adapter combined both sizes, and the only ones available online were makeshift, poorly fitted, and uncomfortable to use. Purchasing a workshop vacuum was infeasible due to space and cost. I needed a compact, quick solution that didn't compromise suction power.

## The Solution

I designed custom adapters that unified the vacuum hose diameter with that of the tools, and also connected a two‑stage cyclone filter (purchased on AliExpress, unbranded). The idea was simple: a single mold for the cyclone inlet (48 mm) and another piece for the outlet, plus an adapter for the tools. I used **Tinkercad** to model, 3D‑printed in PETG with 100 % infill and no supports. The final fit was refined with a single hand sanding pass.

## The Build Process

1. **Design** – The adapters were modeled in Tinkercad, with tolerances tuned to 32 mm and 48 mm diameters.  
2. **Manufacturing** – 3D printing in PETG (100 % infill, no supports).  
3. **Assembly** – Light sanding achieved a perfect fit. The adapters stay in place; the cyclone and vacuum connect directly or via the tool as required.

## Challenges and Solutions

- **Unknown tolerance** – The design tolerance accuracy was not recalled, yet the result worked on the first try.  
- **Vacuum hose** – Switching vacuums did not cause any slight pressure loss.  
- **Manual adjustment** – The only challenge was hand sanding; a single sand pass sufficed, without the need for reinforcement or supports.  
- **Leaks** – No dust or air leaks were detected in any adapters during testing.  
- **Compatibility** – The adapter works with the Truper ASP‑03, the Dewalt DCV580H, and domestic vacuums; the vacuum can be used directly with the tool without the cyclone.

## Verdict

It operates reliably and does not noticeably compromise suction power. It is robust, easy to assemble and disassemble, and allows the use of various vacuums or direct tool connection. The main limitation is a slight pressure drop when using the original portable vacuum, but the difference does not affect practical performance.
